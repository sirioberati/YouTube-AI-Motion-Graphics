Confetti burst

For a win moment (published, done, milestone). The badge pops, then 40 pieces burst out, spin and fall with gravity. Every angle, distance and spin comes from a seeded random, so the burst is the same on every render. Once per video.
Example label.

const r = gsap.utils.random // seeded per piece tl.to(bits, {x: i => seed[i].x, y: i => seed[i].y, rotation: i => seed[i].r, duration: 1.2, ease: "power3.out"}, t) .to(bits, {y: "+=260", opacity: 0, ease: "power1.in"})
