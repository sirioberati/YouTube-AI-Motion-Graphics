# Board types and what each one needs from you

Pick the type from what the lines are doing, not from what looks good. Each type lists the content you must hand the designer. If any input is missing, the board will come out empty or wrong.

Every element on every board gets an **anchor**: the exact words from the script (verbatim, first occurrence on that board) that bring it on screen. Structure (the box, the axis, the outline, the empty receipt) comes in as the board opens; labels, names and numbers wait for their words.

| Type | Use when the lines… | You must provide |
|---|---|---|
| **Headline + hold** | state the board's claim before the detail | the takeaway title; the anchor word that lights its key word |
| **Receipt / itemised list** | add up costs, steps or parts one item at a time | ordered rows (row text, anchor phrase, value if any), the total and its anchor, the order the voice says them in |
| **Comparison bars** | compare amounts (prices, sizes, counts) | each bar's label and value, which values are spoken (anchor) and which are context (shown with structure), the reference line (e.g. the official price) |
| **Anchor diagram** (ladder, pipeline, map, timeline) | carry the backbone idea the video keeps returning to | every zone or rung with its full label and value, which part is lit on this appearance, what was added since the last appearance |
| **Flow diagram** | move money, data, requests or control between parties | the nodes (labels, role lines), the edges (from → to, what moves, which direction), the phrase that introduces each node, the quantity on each edge and its anchor |
| **Side by side** | contrast two states (before/after, official/unofficial, old/new wording) | both sides' contents, the one difference to highlight, the anchor for the highlight |
| **Close read** | quote a document, clause, policy or sentence and dwell on one word | the exact quoted words, the source and date as a small line, the word to highlight and its anchor |
| **Placeholder reveal** | announce a count, then name the members ("three of the twelve…") | the count and its anchor, the names in order with each name's anchor; N grey "?" cards become the real ones when named |
| **Character scene** | explain through a person ("This is Kevin…") | the character's name and one-line role, what they hold or say (speech bubble words), the anchor for their entrance (their name) |
| **Number sticker** | land one money figure or percentage hard | the number as it should print ($500,000), its exact spoken form ("half a million"), the anchor |
| **Evidence** | show proof (a receipt, a result, a screenshot, a test) | what the evidence is, what part to highlight, its honesty tag (SEEN, EXAMPLE, MY READ, SIMPLIFIED) |
| **Hold / breath** | a deliberate pause between boards, or a silent beat | the window (which lines it sits between) and why |

## Numbers: money or count?

A number gets a price sticker only when it is said as money or a rate: it carries "$", "dollars", "percent", or follows pay / costs / at / is about. A count never gets a sticker: "five out of thirteen" must not pop "$13"; "thirteen different prices" is a count. Write each number's role in the brief (money, percent, count, date) so the designer never guesses. A number that returns as context and is not spoken in this board's lines is **recalled**: write "$20 (money, recalled)" and list it in the structure build, never with an anchor.

## Fillers (so nothing goes static)

When the presenter talks for more than about 3 to 4 seconds without anything new landing, plan a filler on that board. A filler is one of:

- a hand-written note of the presenter's exact words (never a paraphrase);
- a sticker with the number just said;
- a small tag (SEEN, EXAMPLE, MY READ);
- a mono receipt line ("NAME ██ PRICE $██").

A filler must echo what is said or add information. It never repeats something already visible and never invents words the presenter did not say.

## When a stretch deserves its own board

Propose a **new board** when more than about 15 seconds of speech covers something the current board does not show, and margin notes would just be sentences. Name it, give its lines and contents, and mark it NEW.
