```

Keep a 480 × 270 box empty in the bottom-right corner (x 1400, y 770) for my camera.
60 px padding on every side; nothing readable outside it.
Titles start at x 60, y 64. Nothing readable inside the camera box, ever.
```

And your end screen:

```

video and a circle for the subscribe button, my community card bottom-left.
```
