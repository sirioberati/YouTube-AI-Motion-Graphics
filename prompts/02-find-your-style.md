# Step 2 · Find your style (Claude Design)

Drop your `references` folder into Claude Design, then say:

```
Here are my references. My use case: boards that explain something while I'm talking
(16:9, for YouTube). I want it to feel: <three words, e.g. calm, sharp, friendly>.
Take the ideas from my references and suggest how I can create something unique
by combining everything together. Give me 3 directions.
```

Go back and forth until it feels like you. This can take days. Don't rush it.
