# Step 4 · Let the AI test itself (QA)

```
QA every board. Open each one in Chrome and screenshot every frame.
Go through every frame and check it against checks.md:
- every readable word is 28 px or more
- nothing readable inside the camera box
- one idea per board, real content only (no invented numbers or logos)
- nothing sits still for more than 5 seconds, nothing is cut off
List every miss with the board, the frame and what's wrong. Then fix it,
and add every new kind of miss to checks.md as a rule.
```
